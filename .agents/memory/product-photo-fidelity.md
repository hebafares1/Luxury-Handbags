---
name: Product photo fidelity
description: User's standing requirement for product imagery in the ROSE collection and interactive viewer.
---

Use the exact collection image for each selected bag in the viewer. Never redraw, generate a replacement, recolor, or tint it. Color swatches require their own real product images.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected placeholder bag models and requested identical product imagery in the collection and viewer.

**How to apply:** Keep future viewer interactions limited to presentation of the existing photo, such as perspective tilt, rather than changing the product's appearance.
