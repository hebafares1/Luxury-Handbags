name: Product photo fidelity
description: User's standing requirement for product imagery in the ROSE collection and interactive viewer.
---

Use the exact collection image as frame 01 for each selected bag. Keep collection photos unchanged; never recolor or tint images. Color swatches require their own real product images.

**Why:** The user rejected placeholder models and unrelated replacement bags. After reviewing reference-generated samples and being warned that hidden views and fine details are inferred, they explicitly approved proceeding with AI approximations for the rotation sequences.

**How to apply:** Additional viewer frames may be AI-generated from the original bag photo, but must be presented as approximate AI views, not verified photographs of the physical bag. The first frame must always remain the collection's original photo.

Numeric angle instructions alone do not reliably produce the intended viewing direction, especially when returning from the side to the front.

**Why:** Generated turntable frames repeatedly showed rear panels where front-facing angles were requested.

**How to apply:** Specify the visible front/back and side faces in words and inspect complete contact sheets rather than trusting frame numbers or angle labels. Even explicit descriptions cannot guarantee exact geometry or a perfectly uniform turntable sequence.
